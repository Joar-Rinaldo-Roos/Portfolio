Master’s thesis · CAISA

Tracking surgical instruments

At Skåne University Hospital in Lund, we asked whether computer vision could help prevent instruments from being left behind during pediatric open-heart surgery.

  • Computer vision
  • YOLO
  • SAM 3
  • Codex

How the results changed the question

The tracking methods we tested fell short of the reliability that task needed. We shifted to a question the data might help answer. Could patterns in which tools surgeons use, and when they switch between them, help them review surgical workflow?

Detection and tracking methods

I worked with YOLO and SAM 3 to detect and track instruments in video. I also explored whether context from the footage could help assess detections and reduce false positives.

How I used Codex

I used Codex to iterate on the instrument-tracking code. The project taught me to judge changes against what the tracker needed to measure and what the footage could actually support.
